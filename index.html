Choosing an AI Companion App

Most AI companion apps look the same in a screenshot.

The differences show up after a week: does the character remember you, does their mood carry over, do they have a life of their own?

Feature lists rarely mention memory depth, which is precisely the thing that determines whether you stay.

Judge a companion app by what it remembers, not by what it renders.
